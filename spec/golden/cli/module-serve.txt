serve — Open something on a port until it is stopped: the Composer, a rendered example, or a static site.

  hecks <Aggregate.command>! [name=value …]    do something
  hecks <Aggregate.query> [name=value …]       read something

commands:
  Server:
    One server opened on a checkout, until it is stopped.
    present!             Serve the banking example's rendered forms and views on a local port until stopped
    compose!             Serve the Composer, a live bluebook editor over one domain directory, on a local port until stopped
    preview!             Serve one directory of static files, such as a website, on a local port until stopped
    serve_query_ir_mcp!  Serve the IR queries to an MCP client over stdio until it closes
    serve_mcp!           Serve the storehouse bus to an MCP client over stdio until it closes (also: mcp!)
  Board:
    One pipeline board served from the Hecks registry, until it is stopped.
    serve!               Show the pipeline board on 127.0.0.1 until stopped, with every bluebook attached to Hecks on its wiring page
