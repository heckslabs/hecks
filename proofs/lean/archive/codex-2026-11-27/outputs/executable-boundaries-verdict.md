## 1. Authorization

**Smallest witness:** One `Banking::Account.Open` with valid command arguments and `role: "Wrong Role"`, from a fresh domain. The ancestral runtime refuses `Unauthorized`, with no state or event effect. The earlier reduced interpreter had no caller input and could not produce that result. [Fixture](boundary-regressions/authorization_min.json).

**Boundary trace:** The caller is an environment input. The ancestral dispatch order checks the role before reference lookup and hydration; a bad role must win over a missing referenced customer. The reduced compiler now emits an inspectable `authorize` rule from the command's declared role. Its evaluator receives the caller role separately from command arguments and refuses before mutation. The corpus fixture with two repeated wrong-role calls followed by the right role also matches, demonstrating the same-state repeatability of this slice.

| Question | Result and status |
|---|---|
| Semantic requirement | **Demonstrated:** declared role plus ambient caller determines admissibility and refusal precedence; refusal leaves state/events unchanged. |
| Execution substrate | **Unresolved:** real actor grants, scopes, provider availability, and authorization across every entry point are outside this interpreter. |
| Relationship expression | **Demonstrated for role strings:** a relation over `(world, caller, command)` yields `Unauthorized` or permits the transition. The caller must remain an explicit input. |
| Construct decision | **Hypothesized:** no new semantic kind is needed; an additional explicit caller-to-role relationship suffices. Live grants need a supplied, versioned authorization observation. |

## 2. Projection refresh

**Smallest witness:** `Customer.Register`, `Account.Open`, `Customer.Suspend`. The third command emits `CustomerSuspended`; the `FreezeAccountsOnSuspension` policy selects that customer's open account, dispatches `FreezeAccount`, and the save seeds `customer_status = "suspended"`. The account becomes frozen and emits `AccountFrozen`. The earlier reduced interpreter left the account open with `customer_status = "active"`. [Fixture](boundary-regressions/projection_refresh_min.json).

**Boundary trace:** The projected field is a **held copy**, not an implicit live dereference. Hecks seeds it on a save and also exposes a separate rebuild sweep. In this witness the policy causes the save that refreshes it. The reduced runner now reads the declared `CustomerSuspended → Account.OpenForCustomer → FreezeAccount` policy, evaluates the query, binds each selected account ID, dispatches the command, and seeds the projection from its declared source at commit. The ancestral `given_first_failure_wins` fixture now matches as well: after the freeze, `ApplyFee` reports the first declared given (`customer is active`) rather than a lifecycle refusal.

| Question | Result and status |
|---|---|
| Semantic requirement | **Demonstrated:** event causality, query selection, first-given order, held projection value, and refresh timing are observable. |
| Execution substrate | **Unresolved:** durable sweep progress, concurrent source changes, transaction isolation, and rebuild recovery are not modeled. |
| Relationship expression | **Demonstrated for this policy:** explicit event-to-query, row-to-command, and source-to-projection relationships reproduce the oracle. |
| Construct decision | **Hypothesized:** no new semantic kind is needed; the correction needs several explicit relationships and a scheduled realization of the resulting command/save. A bare field equality relation is insufficient without timing. |

## 3. Policy and reaction scheduling

Three small witnesses isolate separate scheduling obligations:

| Witness | Shortest fresh-domain trace | Earlier error | Current result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Policy binding](boundary-regressions/policy_binding_min.json) | One `Ledgerbook::Shelf.Stock` | `ShelfNoted` and `Register` were absent. | **Demonstrated exact:** policy `:id` binds the whole composite emitter ID `A:3`, creates `Register#A:3`, and emits `ShelfNoted`. |
| [Per-event order](boundary-regressions/reaction_order_min.json) | `Courier::Run.Start`, then `Advance` | `RunLogged` and `RunNoted` were absent. | **Demonstrated exact:** `Advance` emits `RunLegged` then `RunReported`; the first event's saga emits `RunLogged` before the second event's policy emits `RunNoted`. |
| [Depth bound](boundary-regressions/reaction_depth_min.json) | `Echo::Beacon.Light`, then `Ping` | Only the root ping event appeared. | **Demonstrated exact:** the root plus five reactions emit six `Pinged` events; the next reaction is not dispatched. |

**Boundary trace:** Reaction matching, argument binding, policy-versus-saga order, per-event depth-first scheduling, and a depth limit of five are semantic. The reduced runner compiles policies and saga handlers from ancestral Bluebook IR, interprets `with:` bindings from payload/declared identity heads/row keys, rejects unbound names, and runs each event's policies before its sagas. It does not ask the ancestral dispatcher to execute reduced steps. An explicit `with:` receiver is used to locate the target but is not automatically copied into that command's event payload; an unprojected payload is forwarded. These distinctions were necessary for exact event traces.

| Question | Result and status |
|---|---|
| Semantic requirement | **Demonstrated for these witnesses:** ordered causal reactions, binding scope, and depth cutoff are observable and cannot be treated as implementation detail. |
| Execution substrate | **Unresolved:** outbox persistence, claims, redelivery, idempotency, cross-domain delivery, saga compensation, reaction logs, and failure recovery. The reduced runner is synchronous and in-memory. |
| Relationship expression | **Demonstrated for these declared policy/saga forms:** event-to-ordered-action relationships plus explicit binding and depth state reproduce the frozen observations. |
| Construct decision | **Hypothesized:** scheduling does not require a new domain semantic kind; it requires an additional explicit causal relationship and an ordered evaluator. Durable delivery requires a specialized realization contract and adapter. |

The remaining 15 fixtures stop at their first unsupported operation or output shape. They include correction, cross-domain observations, delegated commands, entity commands, ad hoc queries, nil equality, compensation, some refusal paths, and saga state selection. A blocked fixture is not a pass; later mismatches may appear when it becomes executable. The audit currently compares `instances`, `events`, and `refusals` only; reaction/outbox/saga logs and query answers require explicit comparison in future passes. The current runner handles the policy query and saga-handler forms exercised here, not all Bluebook forms. It uses an in-memory world and does not refine Hecks's transactional outbox.

**Proven:** The earlier Lean files prove abstract relational composition and selected preservation laws under their stated assumptions. No new mechanical proof connects this expanded Ruby evaluator to Hecks.

**Demonstrated:** The five boundary fixtures and 37 completed corpus fixtures match ancestral observable state/event/refusal data exactly.

**Hypothesized:** Function plus inspectable Relationship expressions can cover the remaining executable semantics without a new domain construct.

**Unresolved:** Full 52/52 conformance, closed-language coverage, and physical realization equivalence. The next test should make the 15 blocked fixtures executable and compare reaction, saga, query, and outbox observations before claiming a universal evaluator.
